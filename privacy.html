Privacy Policy — GTA Clips Uploader

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GTA Clips Uploader handles Google account authorization and data when interacting with the YouTube Data API v3.

1. Google OAuth Scopes & Data Usage

GTA Clips Uploader requests the following Google OAuth 2.0 scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — used solely to upload video files to your authorized YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — used solely to set custom thumbnails and video metadata (title, description, tags, and privacy status) for uploads initiated by you.

These permissions are used exclusively at the channel owner's explicit direction to publish content to their own YouTube channel. The application does not access, read, or modify unrelated Google account data.

2. Data Storage & No Third-Party Sharing

  • OAuth refresh/access tokens are stored locally on the user's machine or private authorized runner environment solely to authenticate API upload requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data or Google OAuth tokens with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • No analytics trackers or third-party advertising scripts are embedded on this site.

3. Revocation & Data Deletion

You may revoke GTA Clips Uploader's access at any time via your Google Account Permissions page and delete the local OAuth token file from your system.
